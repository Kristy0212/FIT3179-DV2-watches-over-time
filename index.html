1. Watch History

How did watch brands emerge and evolve throughout their history?

Watch brand founding peaked between 1850 and 1899, with
54 brands in the dataset. This declined to 41 brands
in 1900–1949 and 12 in 1950–1999.

Some long-established watch manufacturers experienced revivals after their original
operations ended, showing how historic watch brands can return
years after becoming defunct.

2. Global Watchmaking

3. Watch Characteristics

How have watch production, sizes, and materials varied over time?

The number of recorded watches is highest in the 2010+
period, particularly for Rolex, suggesting a greater representation of
recent-period watches in this dataset.

Typical watch sizes shift from around 35mm in earlier periods
to 40-41mm after 1990. Before 1950, a second peak
near 50mm suggest greater variation.

Steel dominates several manufacturers, while gold combination and other materials
contribute different shares across brands.
